Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Welcome to Live Cricket Score. This page explains what information this website does and does not collect when you use it.

Your files and inputs stay on your device

Live cricket scores, scorecards, squads, points tables, records and player search. Match data is loaded from public sports data providers (ESPN and CricketData); no user files are involved.

What is counted

Like most websites, we count visits so we can see whether the site is useful. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal information, and a simple view counter that only stores a total number of views per page. No names, email addresses or accounts are involved — this site has no sign-up and no login.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time in Google's Ads Settings (google.com/settings/ads). Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits.

External links

Some pages link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites; please check their own policies.

Children
