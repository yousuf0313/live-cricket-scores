About Live Cricket Score

Live Cricket Score follows international cricket and the major leagues with live scores, match schedules and points tables in one fast, text-first page. During a match the score refreshes automatically about every two minutes, so you can follow the game ball by ball without heavy apps or video eating your data.

Coverage centres on what fans here actually follow: Pakistan’s home and away series, World Cups and other ICC events, and the big franchise leagues — alongside the wider world game. Fixtures, results and standings sit alongside the live card, with all timings shown in Pakistan Standard Time.

Score data is provided by CricketData (cricapi.com). The page is free, needs no sign-up, and is built to stay quick on ordinary phones and slow connections.

How to use it

  1. Open the page during a match — the live match card sits at the top with the current score.
  2. Use the top navigation to jump to World for live and upcoming matches from every league.
  3. Leave the page open: it refreshes itself during matches. There is no need to keep reloading.
  4. Check the match card for venue, series and start-time details before a game begins.

Features

  • Live scores that refresh automatically about every two minutes
  • Upcoming match schedule across series and leagues
  • Points tables for ongoing tournaments
  • Pakistan and world cricket coverage in one place
  • All timings in Pakistan Standard Time (PKT)
  • Lightweight pages that load fast and use little mobile data

Frequently asked questions

How often do the scores update?

The page refreshes its data automatically about every two minutes while matches are on. You do not need to reload manually — just leave the tab open and the score card keeps itself current.

Why is a particular match not shown?

Coverage depends on the data provider. International matches and major leagues are covered reliably; some local and domestic fixtures are not carried by the feed, and very occasionally a match appears a little after the toss.

What time zone are the match timings in?

All times on this site are Pakistan Standard Time (PKT), so a start time you see is the time it begins in Pakistan, wherever in the world the match is played.

Is the score ever behind the TV broadcast?

Online score feeds can trail a broadcast by a short margin, and broadcasts themselves vary. The feed here is a fast, standard score service — good for following the game, not a frame-by-frame companion to television.

Do you offer predictions or betting tips?

No. This site provides scores, fixtures and standings for fans’ information only. It has no betting content, tips or links.

Is the site free to use?

Yes, completely free and with no account. The page is supported by advertising, which keeps live data available to everyone.
