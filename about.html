About Live Cricket Score

Live Cricket Score is a free website for following cricket as it happens: live scores, upcoming schedules and points tables for international series, ICC events and major leagues, with all timings in Pakistan Standard Time.

It is designed for fans who want the score quickly — on a phone, on mobile data, without installing an app or sitting through video. The layout is deliberately text-first and light.

What you can do here

  • Follow live matches with auto-refreshing scores
  • See upcoming fixtures by series and league
  • Check tournament points tables and standings
  • Catch up on results of matches you missed

How it works

Match data comes from CricketData (cricapi.com), a specialist cricket data service. During matches the page requests fresh data about every two minutes and updates the score cards in place, so the page you are looking at is never more than a refresh behind the feed.

Our aim

This site is part of a small family of free tools. The aim is simple: useful tools that open instantly, respect your privacy, work on any phone or computer, and cost nothing to use — with no sign-up and no installation.
